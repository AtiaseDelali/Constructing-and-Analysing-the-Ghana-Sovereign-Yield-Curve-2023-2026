# Results

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| ghana_zero_curves.csv | Fitted zero rates at 12 maturities for all 875 trading days, with Nelson-Siegel parameters, instrument counts and fit error |
| rich_cheap_2026-10-02.csv | Every security on the last day scored against the fitted curve, with the spread in basis points and a rich/cheap/fair flag |
| rate_shock_scenarios.csv | Profit and loss on an equally weighted GHS 1m portfolio of traded bonds under five rate scenarios |

Zero rates are continuously compounded and in percent. Bill yields and bond yields to
maturity elsewhere in the project follow market convention, so they differ slightly from
the zero rate at the same maturity.
